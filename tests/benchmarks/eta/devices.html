Device ETA results

Live measurements from connected devices. Error is predicted finish-time error divided by total solve duration, measured over 10-90% of the run. Results refresh every five seconds.

Below 10 seconds: no accuracy target. 10-30 seconds: up to 50% is acceptable. 30-60 seconds: informational. Above 60 seconds: stripline below 10% (1% preferred), freeform below 25%. Backgrounded runs are flagged; they are not reliable performance comparisons.
